<h1>Overview</h1>
    <p>This Responsive Personal Portfolio is designed to showcase projects and skills professionally and visually appealingly. It adapts to various screen sizes, ensuring an optimal viewing experience.</p>

  <h2>Key Features</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Project Showcase:</strong> Display completed projects with detailed descriptions, images, and links to live demos or repositories.</li>
        <li><strong>Skills Highlight:</strong> Highlight key skills and competencies with visual indicators and concise descriptions.</li>
        <li><strong>Responsive Design:</strong> Ensure seamless viewing across desktops, tablets, and smartphones with a fully responsive layout.</li>
        <li><strong>Contact Information:</strong> Provide easy access to contact details and social media profiles for potential clients or employers.</li>
        <li><strong>Clean and Professional Layout:</strong> Use a modern and clean design to make a strong impression on visitors.</li>
    </ul>
